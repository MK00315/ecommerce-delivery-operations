03 / understand the evidence

/beyond the numbers

Historical associations and experimental ranks.
Clear boundaries for every finding.

Delivery pattern

Longer distances, a +4.89 pp
adjusted late-rate difference.

Baskets above 1,000 km compared with those at 500 km or less. 95% marginal interval: +3.35 to +6.44 percentage points.

How to interpret this association

The comparison uses completed eligible orders, purchase-month adjustment and purchase-week clustered uncertainty. The four exploratory p values use Holm correction. This association does not establish that distance causes the difference.

Experimental model

A useful question.
A modest ranking gain.

Average precision 0.0901, versus a 0.0625 constant baseline. Updated August results reuse an inspected period; absolute probabilities remain unreliable.

Model and validation boundaries

Static logistic regression with 31 inputs, refitted on 83,848 mature observed orders. Diagnostic ROC-AUC: 0.6177. The first independent August experiment failed; the updated result is not a new independent test. There is no real intervention evidence or established production accuracy.

All four adjusted statistical comparisons

Fixed full-cohort evidence. Percentage-point differences adjust for purchase month; marginal intervals cluster by purchase week. Four p values use Holm correction.
